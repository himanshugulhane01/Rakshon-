1. PRODUCT & PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
App / Website Name : Rakshon (Digital Safety Companion)
Hackathon Problem  : Digital Literacy and Cyber Fraud Awareness Assistant for
                     Senior Citizens
Design Theme       : Premium Cyber-Safe Dark Forest Green with Electric Blue
                     and Glowing Cyan Accents
Target Audience    : Senior citizens, caregivers, and family members needing
                     an intuitive, calming, and ultra-safe digital companion.
Core Brand Motto   : "Detect • Block • Learn • Stay Safe"
Mission            : To empower older adults to navigate digital banking, UPI,
                     SMS, and web browsing with total confidence, zero fear,
                     and comprehensive cyber safety.

4. COMPONENT ARCHITECTURE & LAYOUT BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The website / prototype is structured into 6 primary modular layers:

Layer 1: Viewport & Device Frame (.mobile-frame)
- Implements a responsive mobile envelope (width: 100%, max-width: 412px, height: 850px-100vh).
- Renders simulated iOS/Android status bar with live digital clock, Wi-Fi, cellular, and battery.
- Constrained with elegant outer drop shadow and border radius (44px on desktop).

Layer 2: Ambient Geometric Background (.bg-circle-top-right & .bg-circle-bottom-left)
- Two massive, smooth circular graphic shapes positioned off-canvas:
  * Top-Right Circle   : 460px x 460px, top: -120px, right: -160px, opacity: 0.85
  * Bottom-Left Circle : 480px x 480px, bottom: -160px, left: -150px, opacity: 0.85
- Creates an authentic, modern branding aesthetic inspired by Apple, Stripe, and Revolut.

Layer 3: Canvas 2D Cyber Particle Engine (#particleCanvas)
- Hardware-accelerated HTML5 Canvas rendering ambient floating cyber particles.
- Particles drift gently upwards from the bottom, fading in and out organically.
- Interactive radial particle burst triggers whenever the user clicks/taps the shield or when the progress completes.

Layer 4: Stylized Shield-R Cyber Emblem (.shield-wrapper)
- Custom vector artwork featuring the cyber defense shield silhouette with the letter "R" seamlessly integrated into the left boundary curve.
- Tri-color gradient transition: Sky Cyan (#00D2FF) -> Royal Blue (#0084FF) -> Navy (#0038A8).
- Multi-layer visual effects:
  * Soft radiant cyan ambient halo (#38BDF8 with 12px blur).
  * Continuous diagonal specular light gleam sweeping across the emblem every 4 seconds.
  * Zero-G floating oscillation (translating vertically between -5px and +5px).
  * Spring-scale feedback upon user tap/click.

Layer 5: Progress Bar Track & Status Communicator (.progress-section)
- Smooth rounded progress bar track (width: 76%, height: 5px, border-radius: 2.5px).
- Vibrant cyan fill (#38BDF8) with soft neon glow (box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.8)).
- Dynamic status messaging updates as progress fills:
  * 0% - 68%   : "Securing your digital world..."
  * 69% - 95%  : "Verifying threat databases..."
  * 96% - 100% : "Digital Protection Active"

Layer 6: Interactive Action Deck (.bottom-bar)
- Primary CTA button ("Enter Protected Hub") reveals smoothly once protection initialization finishes.
- Native mobile home indicator pill at the base.


5. ANIMATIONS & MOTION ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Zero-G Floating Breathing (@keyframes floatZeroG):
   - Duration : 3.8 seconds
   - Easing   : ease-in-out
   - Iteration: Infinite
   - Motion   : Translates translateY(-5px) to translateY(5px)

2. Specular Metallic Sheen Glint (@keyframes sheenSweep):
   - Duration : 4.0 seconds
   - Easing   : cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
   - Iteration: Infinite
   - Motion   : Translates a 40px gradient glint strip from -180% to +220% across the shield at a 25-degree angle.

3. Cyber Particle Physics:
   - Ambient Count  : 28 active floating nodes
   - Velocity Range : vx = -0.4 to +0.4 px/frame, vy = -0.3 to -1.7 px/frame
   - Life Cycle     : 140 frames with quadratic alpha fade-out
   - Burst Count    : 30-40 particles dispersed radially (360 degrees) upon click or completion
   - Color Palette  : Cyan (#38BDF8), Teal (#2DD4BF), and White (#FFFFFF)


6. SENIOR-FRIENDLY ACCESSIBILITY PRINCIPLES (WCAG 2.1 AA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. High Contrast & Anti-Glare:
   - Deep forest green (#12231B) minimizes glare, ocular fatigue, and blue-light strain for elderly eyes.
   - Text contrast ratios exceed 7:1 for white text on dark forest green, surpassing WCAG AAA standards.
2. Large Touch & Tap Targets:
   - The shield emblem and interactive buttons feature generous hit-boxes (> 48x48dp / > 56px height) to accommodate tremors or reduced motor precision.
3. Unhurried Motion & Cognitive Ease:
   - All animations are smooth, gentle, and non-flashing (safe for photosensitive users).
   - Clear, comforting copy explains exactly what the system is doing without confusing technical jargon.
